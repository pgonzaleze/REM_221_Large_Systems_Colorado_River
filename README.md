# The River That No Longer Reaches the Sea

**REM 221 Systems Thinking and the Environment · Module 10**

A team activity about the Colorado River.You will read a story in tabs, teach your team what you learned, negotiate a draft agreement between the United States and Mexico, and submit it as a PDF.

[▶ Access the app here](https://pgonzaleze.github.io/REM_221_Large_Systems_Colorado_River/) 

## What to do

1. **Form a team** of 4–5 and choose a team name.
2. **Reading (about 5 minutes):** each member takes one tab: **1 River, 2 Rules, 3 Delta, 4 People, 5 Numbers**. Read it and note three things your team must know. In a smaller team, double up.
3. **Teach your team (2 minutes each).** Use the question at the end of your tab.
4. **Open the Decide tab.** Pick a stakeholder role, read your role card, and negotiate as a delegation drafting the next Minute (an addendum to the 1944 U.S.–Mexico Water Treaty). Follow the steps on the page.
5. **Fill in the boxes** in the Decide tab together. Write in full sentences, about 1–3 per box. Your reasoning matters more than matching what really happened.
6. **Save as PDF** (button at the bottom of Decide). In the print window, choose **Save as PDF** as the destination.
7. **Look at "What happened".** That tab stays locked until your instructor gives you the code.

## After the reveal

Enter the code in the **What happened** tab. It compares your team's draft with the real agreement. Mark each row Match, Close or Different, and add a short reflection about the differences. You can save that comparison as a PDF too.

## Submit on Canvas

- Submit **one PDF per team** (the file is named after your team). *(Instructor: edit if your requirement differs.)*
- If asked, also submit the comparison PDF and your short reflection.
- Check that the PDF shows your team name, all team members and every answer before you upload.

## Tips and troubleshooting

- **Answers save only on the device you are using.** Do not clear your browser data, and use the same device for the PDF and for the comparison after the reveal.
- **Check the dropdown boxes** (size of the cut, length of the deal, signatures). If you do not touch them, the first option is submitted.
- **Please do not search for the outcome** before the reveal. Use the facts in the tabs.
- **On a phone,** if the PDF does not save, use the Share button in the print window, then "Save to Files".
- **Print window missing?** Allow pop-ups for the page, or try another browser.
- References are listed in the **Start** tab. The facts come from the cited sources.
